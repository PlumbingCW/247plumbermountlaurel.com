Mount Laurel water and housing, and what they do to your pipes

Three local facts shape common problems here.

Two water suppliers

The Mount Laurel Township Municipal Utilities Authority serves most of the township from deep wells, and New Jersey American Water serves part of it. The township says to assume your tap water is a mix, so ask your utility about your address.

Moderately hard water

The township's water quality summary lists 63 to 138 ppm across the supplies it names. The MUA and New Jersey American Water sit near 80 to 100 ppm. At the high end, scale builds on heating elements and tank bottoms, which shortens water heater life.

Housing from the mid 1960s on

The population went from 5,249 in 1960 to 30,270 in 1990, so the housing is largely modern. Copper supply lines and cast iron or PVC drains are common, and pinhole leaks in older copper are a realistic cause of a spreading ceiling stain.

Lead solder and service lines

Lead solder was allowed in home plumbing through 1982. A 2021 New Jersey law requires water systems to replace lead service lines over roughly a decade, so ask the MUA what material your service line is.
