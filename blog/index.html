Three checks that save a service call

Before you read on or phone anyone, spend two minutes on these.

  • Find the main water shutoff and turn it once so you know it works.
  • Look for the cleanout on your drain line and keep the area clear.
  • Read the date on your water heater label so you know how old the tank is.
  • Check the sump pit lid and float if your home has a basement pump.

None of these needs a tool, and each one makes an emergency call shorter and calmer.
