Calling is quicker than anything else we could offer

A leak, a backup or a dead water heater needs a person on the line, not a message waiting in an inbox. Call (609) 547-1417 and a dispatcher answers, whatever the hour. If water is running, shut off the main valve first if you can reach it safely.

What to have ready when the dispatcher picks up

  • Your street address, the town and the nearest cross street.
  • A short description of what is wrong and where the water is.
  • Which fixtures are slow, leaking or not working.
  • Whether you have already shut off the water, and if the valve turned.
  • The age and type of the water heater, if you know it.
  • The best number to reach you at the house.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and the gas utility from outside before you call us.

When email makes sense, and when it does not

Use email at contact@247plumbermountlaurel.com only for business questions that are not urgent. Do not use it to report a plumbing problem, because nobody can send a plumber from an email thread while water is on the floor.
