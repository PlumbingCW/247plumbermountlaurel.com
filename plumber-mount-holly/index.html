A county seat where the oldest buildings set the tone

The Burlington County Courthouse dates to 1796, and the county prison opened in 1811 and closed in 1965. We cite them only as era markers. Mount Holly was settled in 1677, and the 260-acre Mount Holly Historic District, listed on the National Register in 1973, tells you the town is full of houses and shops from many different decades.

That mix matters under the floor. An old building may have cast iron drains that scale and crack, galvanized steel supply that rusts from the inside, and clay or even brick drain lines under the yard. Expect a plumber here to ask about the age and the materials before quoting, and to want a camera look before opening a wall.

What old pipe does and what usually fixes it

These are general patterns for older buildings. Only an inspection tells you what is in yours.

New Jersey American Water and the Smith Lane main

Mount Holly water supply

Mount Holly is served by New Jersey American Water, and the system was formerly the Mount Holly Water Company. In 2020 the utility replaced about 2,400 feet of 6-inch main on Smith Lane, which runs between Mount Holly and Westampton. That pipe dated as far back as the 1950s, and the new main is 8-inch and 12-inch ductile iron, with five hydrants and 55 utility-owned service lines.

Work like this can stir sediment and trap air in nearby house lines. If you ever see cloudy water or sputtering taps after street work, run a cold tap at the lowest faucet until it clears and clean the aerators. Ask the utility, not your plumber, what your service line is made of. The part from the main to your meter is theirs, and the part inside your wall is ours.

Before we arrive in Mount Holly

Try the old shutoff gently

A valve that has not moved in years can snap or leak at the stem. Turn it slowly by hand. If it will not budge, do not force it, and tell the dispatcher.

Power and old wiring

Older basements often have low outlets and cramped panels. Switch off breakers only if you can reach them dry, and stay out of standing water.

Find the cleanout and floor drain
