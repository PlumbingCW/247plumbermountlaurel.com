Three centuries of houses, and what hides inside their walls

Moorestown was settled by Quakers in 1682, and its First Friends' Meeting House logs go back to about 1700. The Moorestown Historic District covers 47 acres and holds 351 contributing buildings, listed on the National Register in 1990. A house that old may still carry some original cast iron drain stack, galvanized supply pipe, or a clay lateral under the yard.

That matters when you call. Galvanized pipe rusts from the inside, so low pressure at one fixture can point to a narrowed line rather than a bad faucet. A cast iron stack can crack or scale over and slow every drain on the floor above it. A plumber in an older Moorestown house starts by finding out which materials are actually there.

Where Moorestown's tap water comes from

Moorestown runs its own water system through the Department of Public Works instead of buying everything from a large utility. The 2020 water quality report lists 7 wells in the Potomac-Raritan-Magothy aquifer, blended with Delaware River water the township buys from New Jersey American Water. The plants on North Church Street and Hartford Road were upgraded and put online in 2020, and the Kings Highway plant in April 2018.

Because the source is a blend, water at your tap can change in taste or chlorine smell over a season. That is not a plumbing fault by itself. If a new smell shows up at only one faucet, look at that fixture and its aerator first, and call us if cleaning does not fix it.

Lead in the service line, and the question to ask Public Works

The 2020 report shows lead 90th percentile results of 2.3 and 4.3 parts per billion, both well under the 15 ppb action level. A 2021 New Jersey law also requires water systems to replace lead service lines over roughly a decade. Neither fact tells you what your own line is made of.

Ask the Moorestown water department what material your service line is, especially in an older house. If you are planning a water line repair or any work where the line is exposed, that answer changes the plan. Lead solder was also allowed in home plumbing through 1982, so a house from before then deserves a look at its joints.

Before we arrive at your Moorestown house

Find the main shutoff

In an older Moorestown house it is often in the basement near the front wall, and the valve may be stiff. Turn it slowly, and do not force it if it will not move.

Kill power near water

If water is on the floor near outlets, a basement panel or a finished lower level, switch off that breaker from a dry spot. If you have to stand in water to reach it, stay out and call.

Clear the area

Move rugs and boxes from the wet zone. Old plaster ceilings can sag when soaked, so keep people from standing under a stain.

Note what you see

Write down which fixtures are slow, whether the water is rusty, and the age of the heater if you know it. It helps the plumber bring the right parts.
