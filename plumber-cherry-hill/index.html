Backups, bursts and dead water heaters in a township of 74,553

With 74,553 residents in the 2020 count, Cherry Hill is a large township with a lot of plumbing in it. The urgent problems that cannot wait are a toilet and tub backing up together, a pipe spraying inside a wall, and a water heater that has let go on the floor. The first two usually mean the main line or a supply pipe, not a single fixture.

If every drain on the lowest level gurgles at once, stop running water and call. A sewer line cleaning clears a blockage, but a camera decides whether the cause was grease, roots or a broken pipe. We say plainly that we cannot know until the camera goes in.

Mall-era houses and the pipes that came with them

Cherry Hill was incorporated in 1844 as Delaware Township and took its current name in 1961, the year Cherry Hill Mall opened. Population went from 10,358 in 1950 to 64,395 in 1970. Most of its housing therefore went up in that boom, so copper supply lines, cast iron or later plastic drains, and tank heaters near the end of their life are common.

In practice, that means pinhole leaks in aging copper and rusted-out tank bottoms are realistic causes of a spreading stain or a wet floor. When a heater is past its prime, water heater installation of a new unit is often cleaner than another repair.

Kresson Road main work and what it can do to your faucets

New Jersey American Water announced on February 2, 2026 that it would replace an older cement water main on Kresson Road with a new 16-inch ductile iron main. The job covers nearly a mile between Springdale Road and Cropwell Road, with completion scheduled for June 2026. Main work like that can stir sediment loose.

If you live near a project like this and your water turns cloudy or a faucet sputters, run the cold tap for a few minutes and then clean the aerator. A clogged aerator is a simple fix. Call us if the flow stays weak after that, since a faucet repair or a closer look at your own supply line may be needed.

Before we arrive in Cherry Hill

Shut the water at the main

It is often near the water meter or where the line enters the basement or utility room. Turn the valve clockwise until it stops.

Cut power to wet areas

For water near a laundry area, panel or finished lower level, switch off the breaker if you can reach it dry. If the floor is wet around the panel, do not touch it and call.

Stop using drains

During a backup, hold off on the washer, dishwasher and showers. Every gallon you send down can come back out the lowest drain.

Note the details

Write down your neighborhood, which fixtures are affected and whether the smell is sewage or just damp. Take a photo of the heater label if it is leaking.
