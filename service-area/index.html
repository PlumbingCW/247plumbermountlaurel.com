Where the 24/7 line starts and where it reaches

The dispatch address is in Mount Laurel, a Burlington County township of about 22 square miles with a single ZIP code, 08054. From there the line serves the neighboring townships along Route 38, Route 73, I-295 and the New Jersey Turnpike. Nights, weekends and holidays are covered the same way as daytime.

We do not promise an arrival time for any town, because the plumber's earlier calls and the roads change it. What we can say is that the dispatcher will tell you honestly whether your address is in range. Call (609) 547-1417 and give the street and the nearest cross street.

Bordering towns versus nearby towns

Mount Laurel borders nine municipalities. They are Evesham, Hainesport, Lumberton, Maple Shade, Medford, Moorestown, Westampton and Willingboro in Burlington County, plus Cherry Hill in Camden County. Evesham, Medford, Moorestown and Cherry Hill have pages here for that reason.

Mount Holly is nearby, not bordering. It sits to the northeast, past Westampton and Lumberton, and has its own page because it is the county seat and a common address. If you are closer to the line between two towns, call and the dispatcher will sort out which plumber is nearest.

Willingboro and Maple Shade border Mount Laurel but do not have separate pages yet. Calls from there are welcome, so call to confirm. The same goes for Hainesport, Lumberton and Westampton.
